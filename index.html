01 About

I'm the person you call when something's broken.

Eight years of hands-on technology work across HP, Kroger, Best Buy, and Spectrum taught me how systems actually fail in the wild — not how they're supposed to. I just finished my B.S. in Software Engineering at WGU, and I'm looking for SDET and QA Automation work where that instinct for "wait, what about this edge case?" is the job, not a side quest.

I write TypeScript/JavaScript and C#, build automated test suites, and care a lot about the difference between code that passes and code that holds. Outside of work I produce music, do voice acting, build Gundams, and watch the sky. The two halves are more related than they look — both are about noticing small things most people miss.
